Demystifying Probability Distributions for 1st Year Students:

Many freshmen ask how to quickly decide between a Binomial and a Poisson distribution during practical exams. Remember: If trials n are fixed with constant probability p, use Binomial. If counting occurrences over a continuous interval of time or space, think Poisson!
